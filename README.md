# Dil Se... For You ♡

An interactive romantic apology website designed with love.

## ✨ Features
- **Vintage Handmade Parchment Envelope**: Authentic craft paper folds, vintage postage stamp, and classic crimson 3D wax seal.
- **Dual Opening Mechanism**: Tap the wax seal directly or slide the vintage heart slider track to open.
- **Cinematic Unfold Animation**: Calm, elegant 2-stage paper emergence without distracting confetti or sparkles.
- **Seamless Romantic Background Audio**: Automatically plays `Song.mp4` with a silky fade-in upon opening, with zero visible player controls.
- **Multi-Step Chain Surprises**:
  1. Cute GIF Carousel with sweet affection.
  2. Image card surprise with `image.jpg`.
  3. Video surprise featuring `DUDUBUBU.mp4` with intelligent audio coordination (pauses background music while video plays and resumes upon close).
